Refinancing

Is your current loan still working for you?

A comprehensive audit of your lending structure to see whether it still matches your goals in 2026.

The immediate gain: an interest rate audit

Lending markets move quickly. I compare your current interest rate against what's currently available from other lenders to identify genuine savings — sometimes a straightforward renegotiation with your existing bank is enough; sometimes switching lenders makes more sense.

The longer-term play: an equity strategy

Refinancing isn't only about the rate — it's about what your equity can do for you. If you've built up equity, we look at whether it makes sense to redeploy some of it toward an investment purchase or debt consolidation, as part of a longer-term plan rather than a one-off switch.
