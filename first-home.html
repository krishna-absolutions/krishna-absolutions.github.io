Navigating the Brisbane market as a first buyer

Stepping into the property market for the first time can feel overwhelming — real estate agents, open homes, and unfamiliar banking terminology all at once. As your local Brisbane mortgage broker, I cut through that noise to find a loan structure that actually fits your income, deposit and timeline.

Queensland government concessions worth checking

Depending on your circumstances, you may be eligible for:

  • Queensland First Home Owner Grant — financial support toward buying or building a new home, subject to eligibility criteria.
  • First home transfer duty concession — reduced or waived stamp duty on eligible homes under the relevant threshold, cutting your upfront cash requirement.

Grant amounts and thresholds change periodically — I'll confirm the current figures and your eligibility as part of our conversation.
