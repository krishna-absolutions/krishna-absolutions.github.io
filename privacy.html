Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

At AB Loan Solutions, your privacy and the security of your financial data are a priority. This policy outlines how information you provide to us is collected, used and protected.

1. Information we collect

We may collect personal information necessary to assist with your mortgage or lending inquiry, including:

  • Contact details (name, email, phone number)
  • Financial information relevant to loan serviceability and credit assessments
  • Details required for identity verification under Australian regulations

2. How we use your information

Information is used to provide mortgage broking services, to communicate with lenders on your behalf during an application, and to meet legal and regulatory obligations.

3. Data security

We take reasonable steps to keep your data secure and do not sell or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

4. Your rights

You can request access to the personal information we hold about you, and ask for corrections if it's inaccurate.

5. Contact us

Questions about privacy can be sent to krishna@abloansolutions.com.au.
