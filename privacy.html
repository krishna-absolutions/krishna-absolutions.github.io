Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

At AB Loan Solutions, your privacy and the security of your financial data are our top priorities. This policy outlines how we collect, use, and protect the information you provide to us.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect personal information necessary to assist with your mortgage or lending inquiries, including:

  • Contact details (name, email, phone number).
  • Financial information relevant to loan serviceability and credit assessments.
  • Details required for identity verification in accordance with Australian regulations.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information solely for the purpose of:

  • Providing professional mortgage broking and financial strategy advice.
  • Communicating with lenders on your behalf during the application process.
  • Complying with legal and regulatory obligations.

3. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to ensure your data is stored securely. We do not sell or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

4. Your Rights

You have the right to request access to the personal information we hold about you and to ask for corrections if the information is inaccurate.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding your privacy, please contact us directly at krishna@abloansolutions.com.au.
