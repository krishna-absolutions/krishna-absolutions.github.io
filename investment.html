Avoiding the borrowing wall

Many investors hit a "borrowing wall" because their first loan wasn't structured with future purchases in mind. I work with you to set up multi-lender structures that avoid unnecessary cross-collateralisation, so your existing assets stay protected as you acquire new ones.

Finance that fits your broader wealth plan

Your property portfolio is usually one part of a bigger picture. Whether you're balancing it against shares, superannuation or other assets, I help structure your debt so your overall cash flow supports the plan — not just the next purchase in isolation.
